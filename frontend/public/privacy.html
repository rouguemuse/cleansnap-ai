CleanSnap Studio — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Publisher: V Wilder (Context & Muse)

1. 100% On-Device Image Processing

CleanSnap Studio processes your images, subject cutouts, manual edge refinements, studio backdrop composites, and vector-clipped .SVG / .PNG exports locally on your device using WebAssembly and HTML5 Canvas. Your photos are never sold, shared with third parties, or used to train external machine learning models.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

  • We do not require user accounts, logins, or passwords.
  • We do not collect, store, or transmit personal identifiers, location data, contacts, or payment information.
  • We do not embed third-party advertising trackers or behavioral analytics SDKs.

3. Contact

For support or privacy inquiries regarding CleanSnap Studio, visit https://bold-oppenheimer.vercel.app.
