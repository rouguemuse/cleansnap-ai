PART 01 · 60-SECOND QUICK-START WORKFLOW

How to Cut Out Subjects, Stage Backdrops & Export Vector .SVG Cutlines

  1. Drop Photos or a Batch Folder: In the left sidebar under Step 1: Add Images, click + Photos (for individual files) or + Folder (to ingest an entire inventory directory of up to 200 images). All segmentation runs 100% locally on your device using the embedded U²-Net ONNX neural engine.
  2. Select Your Studio Backdrop: Under Step 2: Studio Environment, choose from the 5 built-in environments (01 Green Screen, 02 Corkboard, 03 Belgian Slate Chalkboard, 04 Sunny Grass Yard, or 05 Alpha Cutout .PNG/.SVG) or any unlocked Industry Backdrop Pack (06–11).
  1. Inspect or Refine Cutout Edges: Drag the Before / After Slider in the center stage (or use ← / → arrow keys) to inspect edge fidelity. Click Refine Edges (Brush) to paint/erase mask pixels at up to 400% zoom.
  2. Export Master Assets: Click Download Master (.JPG / .PNG) for full-resolution imagery, Vector-Clipped .SVG for die-cut sticker paths, or switch to the 03 Export Vault tab to download a single batch .ZIP archive.

PART 02 Sub-Pixel Edge Brush Refiner

When working with intricate hair, fur, translucent glass, or complex product straps, switch the stage header from Before / After Slider to Refine Edges (Brush):

  • Restore Subject (+ Brush): Paints opaque foreground alpha back into areas that were over-trimmed.
  • Erase Background (- Eraser): Removes trapped background pockets (e.g., between chair legs or wheel spokes).
  • Ruby Mask Inspection Mode: Overlays a high-contrast crimson tint over removed background pixels so you can spot stray 1px artifacts immediately.
  • Morphological Choke / Expand (-12px to +12px): Tightens or expands the entire silhouette perimeter uniformly.
  • RGB Halo Decontamination (0%–100%): Purges green-screen or bright driveway color spill from semi-transparent edge pixels.

PART 04 Using Vector-Clipped .SVG Cutlines

Every processed photo automatically generates a mathematical Marching-Squares vector contour embedded inside an industry-standard .SVG file:

  • Figma & Adobe Illustrator: Drag the exported _vector_clipped.svg directly onto your canvas. The outer <clipPath> path is an editable cubic Bézier/polygon vector curve.
  • Cricut Design Space, Silhouette & Roland VersaWorks: Use the vector path as a kiss-cut or die-cut sticker contour line around the embedded high-resolution transparent PNG.
  • Batch .ZIP Export Vault: Open 03 Export Vault in the top navigation bar, type a custom folder name, and click Export Batch Archive (.ZIP) to package all completed cutouts at once.
